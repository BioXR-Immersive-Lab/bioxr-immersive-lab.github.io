HandXplorer AR

Description

The anatomy of the hand is challenging because it contains many small structures arranged in a complex three-dimensional configuration. Traditional illustrations and physical models do not provide an intuitive way to isolate, manipulate and examine each bone interactively.

This project develops an image-tracking augmented-reality application. The user points the device at a reference image of a hand and views a complete 3D hand model overlaid on it. Individual bones can be selected, highlighted, labeled and isolated. Users may enlarge a selected bone to inspect details or return to the full model.

Objectives

  • Display a complete 3D hand model in AR over a reference image.
  • Highlight individual bones and display their names and functions.
  • Allow users to isolate, enlarge and restore each bone structure.
  • Provide a more interactive alternative to traditional illustrations and physical models.

Technology

🖥️ Hardware

XREAL Ultra 2.

⚙️ Software

Unity3D + XREAL SDK.
