KinectCalib

Description

KinectCalib is a research project focused on advanced calibration and body-tracking enhancement for Kinect-based applications. Its purpose is to increase anatomical registration accuracy in augmented reality, biomechanics and anatomical visualization.

The project combines two calibration systems. The first corrects Kinect landmark estimates in critical regions, especially the shoulders and hips, to produce a more accurate and consistent skeleton. The second improves spatial registration between the detected skeleton and 3D anatomical models, aligning virtual structures with the hands, legs and other body segments.

The resulting algorithms provide a reusable technology platform for future laboratory projects involving Kinect in biomedical engineering, biomechanics, rehabilitation and medical education.

Objectives

  • Correct Kinect landmark estimates in critical anatomical regions such as the shoulders and hips.
  • Improve spatial registration between the detected skeleton and 3D anatomical models.
  • Provide a reusable calibration platform for future Kinect-based laboratory projects.
  • Increase body-tracking accuracy for biomechanics, rehabilitation and medical-education applications.

Technology

⚙️ Software
