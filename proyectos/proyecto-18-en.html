KineMind

Description

KineMind is an immersive neurocognitive and motor rehabilitation system that combines virtual reality with haptic feedback to improve memory and motor coordination through interactive exercises.

The system has two levels. In Level 1, users memorize the order of visual figures and reorganize them after they reappear randomly. After completing three sequences, they advance to Level 2, where they trace a path of points using hand movement. A haptic band vibrates immediately when an error in the trajectory is detected.

Objectives

  • Train sequential memory through recognition and reordering exercises.
  • Train motor coordination through guided drawing and trajectory-following tasks.
  • Provide immediate haptic feedback when errors occur during motor exercises.
  • Combine cognitive and motor training in a single immersive experience.

Technology

🖥️ Hardware

Meta Quest 3 + custom haptic band with ESP32, BNO085 sensor and vibration motor.

⚙️ Software

Unity3D + Meta XR SDK.
