SpiroApp — Respiratory Biofeedback for Home Pulmonary Rehabilitation

Description

Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease is a major global cause of death. In mild stages, inspiratory training can help reduce dyspnea, but home therapy often lacks supervision, leading to low adherence or incorrect execution. Objective tools are needed to verify slow inspiration, target volume and breath holding while monitoring fatigue and oxygen desaturation.

SpiroApp is a portable ESP32-based system using a Sensirion SDP810 differential-pressure sensor to estimate respiratory flow and volume. A BLE mobile application guides the user with visual and auditory biofeedback. A MAX30102 pulse oximeter monitors SpO2 during the maneuver, and Firebase stores session history to support remote follow-up.

Objectives

  • Measure differential pressure and convert it into respiratory flow and accumulated volume.
  • Monitor SpO2 using the MAX30102 as a physiological safety measure.
  • Guide users autonomously through interactive visual and auditory biofeedback over BLE.
  • Store session progress in the cloud to support remote monitoring and telerehabilitation.

Technology

🖥️ Hardware

ESP32, Sensirion SDP810 differential-pressure sensor, MAX30102 pulse oximeter, buzzer, EasyEDA-designed PCB and ergonomic 3D-printed enclosure with pneumatic separation.

⚙️ Software

SpiroApp mobile application developed in Flutter with interactive curves and alerts, BLE communication and Firebase session-history storage.
