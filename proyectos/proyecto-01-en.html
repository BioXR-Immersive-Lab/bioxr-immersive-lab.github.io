Anatomy Education 🔧 In Development

Magic Mirror – Upper Limb

Description

Most Magic Mirror applications in medicine are limited to central body regions such as the head, neck and torso, where the augmented-reality window remains anchored. This limitation prevents the full body from being used as an interactive surface, especially the upper limbs, where detailed anatomical visualization is essential for education.

The project proposes an advanced tracking algorithm that allows the Magic Mirror AR window to adapt dynamically to the complete movement of the upper limb, from the shoulder to the fingers, while displaying anatomy over the user’s own body in real time. This approach can increase learning motivation and improve knowledge retention.

Objectives

  • Develop an advanced tracking algorithm for the entire upper limb (shoulder → fingers).
  • Enable the AR window to follow arm movement dynamically through a responsive path.
  • Extend the use of the body as an interactive surface beyond the head, neck and torso.
  • Increase motivation and retention in anatomy learning through real-time overlaid visualization.

Technology

🖥️ Hardware

Azure Kinect + PC + monitor frontal.

⚙️ Software

Unity3D + Azure Kinect SDK.
