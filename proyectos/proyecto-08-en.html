Anatomy Education 🔧 In Development

Virtual Dissection Hall (VAM)

Description

Access to anatomy laboratories, cadavers and physical models is limited at many universities. Traditional atlases, illustrations and static models can make three-dimensional spatial understanding difficult and provide limited opportunities for repeated hands-on practice.

Virtual Anatomy Mirror (VAM) is an immersive virtual-reality application for Oculus Quest 2 inspired by the magic-mirror concept used in advanced anatomy education. Students enter a virtual dissection hall from any location and explore a life-size digital cadaver through a head-tracked viewing window.

Objectives

  • Provide remote access to a virtual dissection hall containing a life-size digital cadaver.
  • Support guided exploration by anatomical system, including skeletal, muscular, nervous and vascular systems.
  • Display abdominal surgical access points and related medical images.
  • Include gamified assessment tasks such as identifying structures, tracing the median nerve and locating surgical landmarks.

Technology

🖥️ Hardware

Oculus Quest 2 + Insta360.

⚙️ Software

Unity + Meta XR SDK.
