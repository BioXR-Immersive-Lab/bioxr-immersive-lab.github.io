BYMAX — Home Monitoring for Older Adults with Acute Respiratory Infections

Description

Acute respiratory infections are a major public-health concern because of their high incidence and potential to progress to respiratory complications in older adults. In Loreto, limited access to healthcare facilities makes periodic home monitoring difficult and may delay detection of clinical deterioration.

BYMAX is a compact 20 × 10 × 10 cm PLA-printed device for monitoring older adults with acute respiratory infections in hard-to-reach areas. HealthyPi 5 measures ECG, SpO2, PPG and body temperature. Data are transmitted by Bluetooth to a mobile application for real-time visualization and history review, while RGB LEDs and a buzzer generate alerts when the system detects deterioration relative to the patient’s baseline.

Objectives

  • Monitor ECG, SpO2, PPG and body temperature using HealthyPi 5.
  • Transmit data by BLE to an ESP32 that manages alerts and compares measurements with the patient’s baseline.
  • Display real-time physiological signals and historical data in a Bluetooth-connected mobile application.
  • Address electrical safety, usability, cybersecurity and risk-management requirements based on IEC 60601, IEC 62366-1, IEC 81001-5-1, ISO 14971 and ISO 13485.

Technology

🖥️ Hardware

HealthyPi 5 with AFE4400 PPG/SpO2, MAX30205 temperature and MAX30001 ECG/respiration; ESP32; AHT10 sensor; custom power, step-up and USB-C charging PCB; 3D-printed PLA enclosure.

⚙️ Software

ESP32 firmware for trend analysis and alert management, plus a Bluetooth mobile application with cloud-based history storage.
