ThorAX

Description

Medical students often struggle to correlate three-dimensional physical anatomy with its two-dimensional representation in radiographs, CT scans and MRI. This gap between the real body and radiological images can limit diagnostic interpretation and clinical understanding.

ThorAX is a gesture-controlled medical-image viewer for the thorax and abdomen using Kinect v2. The right hand navigates CT, MR or X-ray slices, while the left hand selects the imaging modality and anatomical plane through a floating radial menu. The interface includes one main image plane, two secondary reference planes, predefined organ segmentations and optional 3D reconstructions.

Objectives

  • Navigate CT, MR and X-ray slices using right-hand gestures.
  • Select modality and axial, coronal or sagittal planes with the left hand through a radial menu.
  • Display one primary plane and two secondary reference planes simultaneously.
  • Show predefined organ segmentations and their 3D reconstructions.

Technology

🖥️ Hardware

Kinect v2 + PC + front-facing monitor.

⚙️ Software

Unity3D + Azure Kinect SDK + Blender.
