VetSim: Canine Anesthesia and Surgery

Description

Practical training in veterinary anesthesia requires safe operation of equipment such as anesthesia machines and infusion pumps. Training with real patients involves risk and limits the number of times students can repeat each procedure.

VetSim is a virtual-reality simulator for the Faculty of Veterinary Medicine and Animal Science. It recreates the operation of an anesthesia machine and infusion pump during canine surgery, allowing students to practice equipment handling safely and repeatedly.

Objectives

  • Simulate operation of an anesthesia machine during canine surgery.
  • Simulate use of an infusion pump during the same procedure.
  • Enable safe and repeatable practice without risk to real patients.

Technology

🖥️ Hardware

Meta Quest 3.

⚙️ Software
