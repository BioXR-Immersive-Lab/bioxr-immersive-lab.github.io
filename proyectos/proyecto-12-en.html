TransfusionVR

Description

Blood-component transfusion is a common hospital procedure, but it involves risks associated with human error, adverse reactions and failures in the transfusion process. Traditional training is largely theoretical and offers limited opportunities to practice rare or high-risk scenarios safely and repeatedly.

TransfusionVR is an immersive clinical simulator for Meta Quest 3 that recreates a complete hospital environment. Students train in multiplayer sessions with clinical roles such as physician, nurse and medical technologist, strengthening communication and teamwork while receiving immediate feedback on their decisions.

The project is developed in collaboration with Hospital Nacional Cayetano Heredia — Blood Bank Service.

Objectives

  • Request and verify blood components by confirming patient identity, ABO/Rh compatibility and a virtual QR-coded wristband.
  • Perform the transfusion procedure, including venous access, component administration and vital-sign monitoring.
  • Practice appropriate blood-component prescribing based on a virtual clinical history, with immediate feedback and explanation of clinical risk.
  • Recognize and respond to simulated adverse reactions such as fever, urticaria, dyspnea and hypotension.
  • Detect and correct simulated errors, including incorrect units and patient misidentification, before transfusion begins.

Technology

🖥️ Hardware

Meta Quest 3.

⚙️ Software

Unity + Meta XR SDK.
