NeuroPlan AR — Preoperative Planning in Neurosurgery

Description

This project adapts an existing augmented-reality system—originally developed for pedicle-screw placement in spinal surgery—into a preoperative planning platform for neurosurgery. It builds on the BSD-3-Clause work by Pose-Díez-de-la-Lastra et al. published in IJCARS in 2023, replacing screw-specific logic with neurosurgical planning functions.

Using HoloLens 2, the system allows neurosurgeons to visualize 3D models of the brain, tumor, ventricles and other structures; navigate MRI images in real time; perform measurements; and plan the surgical approach. The architecture connects HoloLens 2 for holographic visualization, Unity on a PC as the central hub through Holographic Remoting, and 3D Slicer for image processing, segmentation and measurement. Unity and Slicer communicate through OpenIGTLink over TCP/IP.

Objectives

  • Visualize pre-segmented patient-specific anatomy, including the brain, tumor, ventricles, skull and vessels, with independent visibility and anatomical color coding.
  • Navigate 2D MRI slices in real time using a movable cutting plane and selectable sequences such as T1+Gd, T2, FLAIR and MRA.
  • Overlay 3D Slicer segmentations in color on MRI images displayed in HoloLens.
  • Display measurements including skin-to-tumor distance, tumor dimensions and volume, and distances to ventricles and critical vessels.
  • Allow clinicians to plan the surgical approach by drawing points directly on the 3D brain model with a virtual pen based on MRTK raycasting.

Technology

🖥️ Hardware

Microsoft HoloLens 2; Windows 10/11 PC with a dedicated GPU and at least 16 GB RAM; both devices connected to the same network, preferably 5 GHz Wi-Fi.

⚙️ Software

Unity 2021.3.9f1, MRTK 2.8.x, 3D Slicer 5.0.3 or later with SlicerIGT and OpenIGTLink extensions, Visual Studio, and Holographic Remoting Player.
