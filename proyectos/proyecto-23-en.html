SAMIWALK — Objective Assessment of the Six-Minute Walk Test

Description

Population ageing increases the prevalence of cardiopulmonary disease and reduces locomotor capacity, altering gait and functional performance. The six-minute walk test is widely used, but it requires several instruments and may include subjective components.

SAMIWALK provides an objective measurement tool that complements the six-minute walk test in older adults with cardiopulmonary conditions. It analyzes gait and relates the measurements to the Physiological Cost Index, calculated from walking heart rate, resting heart rate and walking speed. This index offers an early objective indicator of gait efficiency for home and clinical rehabilitation.

Objectives

  • Support physiotherapists with objective measurements of physiological cost during the six-minute walk test.
  • Assess gait and correlate it with Physiological Cost Index values to estimate the energy cost of walking.
  • Perform real-time monitoring and processing during walking.
  • Transmit data and support remote visualization.

Technology

🖥️ Hardware

HealthyPi 5 with MAX30001 single-lead ECG, BNO086 IMU, display, lithium battery and separated front/rear enclosure. Modular architecture using direct Qwiic and I2C connections.

⚙️ Software

Zephyr RTOS firmware and a cross-platform React Native interface that calculates the Physiological Cost Index from heart rate and Z-axis speed data.
