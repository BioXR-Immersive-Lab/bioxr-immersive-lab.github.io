Anatomy Education 🔧 In Development

OculusLearn Anatomy

Description

Traditional anatomy education often relies on passive methods such as textbook memorization and static models. These approaches can limit spatial understanding, knowledge retention and student engagement. Correctly identifying multiple anatomical structures in three dimensions remains especially challenging for beginners.

OculusLearn Anatomy is an immersive virtual-reality educational tool for Oculus Quest 2 that supports active, gamified learning of human anatomy. Students can explore 3D anatomical structures—including bones, muscles and nerves—while accessing contextual information. The application includes a free exploration mode and a serious game in which users identify highlighted body structures.

Objectives

  • Enable free exploration of 3D anatomical structures with contextual information.
  • Assess anatomical identification through a serious game.
  • Promote active and gamified learning in an immersive VR environment.
  • Improve spatial understanding and retention of anatomical knowledge.

Technology

🖥️ Hardware

Oculus Quest 2 VR + Insta360.

⚙️ Software

Unity3D + Meta XR SDK.
