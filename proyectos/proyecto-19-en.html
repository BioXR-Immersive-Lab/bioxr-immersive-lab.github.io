Vib-AR — Therapy for Phantom Limb Pain

Description

Phantom limb pain can persist for years and many affected individuals do not seek professional treatment. Vib-AR aims to create a low-cost, safe and user-friendly prototype combining visual therapy through augmented reality with vibration therapy.

MediaPipe uses a webcam and PC to display a virtual hand over the residual limb. A BNO085 motion sensor detects when the user exceeds a predefined movement threshold, triggering an animation in which the virtual arm rises and creates the illusion that the missing limb is moving. This mirror-therapy-inspired visual stimulus is complemented by a vibration motor integrated into a flexible TPU band.

Objectives

  • Detect residual-limb movement using the BNO085 sensor.
  • Overlay a virtual hand or arm on the residual limb using MediaPipe.
  • Trigger a rising-arm animation when the detected movement exceeds a defined threshold.
  • Complement visual therapy with vibration generated by an Arduino-controlled motor.
  • Combine visual and vibratory stimulation as a multimodal approach for phantom limb pain.

Technology

🖥️ Hardware

Webcam + PC, BNO085 sensor, Arduino Nano, vibration motor and custom flexible TPU band.

⚙️ Software

MediaPipe + Arduino firmware for BNO085 sensing and vibration-motor control.
