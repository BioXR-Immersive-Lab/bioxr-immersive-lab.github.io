Anatomy Education 🔧 In Development

AR-Body Atlas Mobile

Description

Learning the anatomy of internal organs often requires costly physical models, specialized software or high-end augmented-reality devices such as HoloLens. This creates a barrier for institutions with limited resources and for independent learning outside the classroom. Students may also struggle to locate thoracic and abdominal organs in relation to the real body surface.

AR-Body Atlas Mobile is an augmented-reality application for smartphones and tablets that overlays 3D models of thoracic and abdominal organs—including the heart, lungs, liver, stomach, kidneys and intestines—directly onto a person’s body in real time. MediaPipe is used to align the models with the user.

Objectives

  • Overlay 3D thoracic and abdominal organs on the real body using only a smartphone.
  • Align anatomical models accurately using MediaPipe.
  • Reduce the cost barrier associated with high-end AR devices.
  • Support independent anatomy learning outside the classroom.

Technology

🖥️ Hardware

Smartphone or tablet running iOS or Android.

⚙️ Software

Unity3D + MediaPipe.
