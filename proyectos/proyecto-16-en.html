Surgery 🔧 In Development

LaparoView VST — Meta Quest 3

Description

During conventional laparoscopic surgery, the surgeon views the procedure on an external monitor located on the surgical tower. Clinicians at Clínica Anglo Americana have begun exploring augmented- and mixed-reality headsets during laparoscopic procedures and have tested an initial workflow in several surgeries.

LaparoView VST recreates this functionality through a custom Unity application for Meta Quest 3, a video-see-through mixed-reality headset. The real environment is captured by cameras and displayed together with virtual content, allowing a large virtual laparoscopic screen to be positioned above the patient or within the surgeon’s preferred field of view.

The project is developed in collaboration with Clínica Anglo Americana — Laparoscopic Surgery Service.

Objectives

  • Develop a Unity application that receives and displays the laparoscopic tower video signal inside the headset.
  • Generate a large virtual display that can be positioned according to the surgeon’s needs.
  • Evaluate the VST-HMD approach in terms of latency, visual comfort and video quality.
  • Progressively incorporate patient medical images and 3D anatomical models beside the surgical field.
  • Validate the workflow in a supervised clinical environment with Clínica Anglo Americana.

Technology
