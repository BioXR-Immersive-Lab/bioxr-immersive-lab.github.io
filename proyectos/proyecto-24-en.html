S-REHAB — sEMG and Kinematic Monitoring in Upper-Limb Rehabilitation

Description

Rehabilitation often lacks an objective and quantitative method for monitoring movement execution, resulting in dependence on visual observation and manual estimation. In subacromial impingement syndrome, inadequate tracking of range of motion and compensatory upper-trapezius recruitment can compromise safe conservative treatment.

S-REHAB is a modular wireless wearable using a BLE star topology to eliminate intermodule cables and reduce motion artifacts. It includes two synchronized acquisition nodes: an arm module with an ESP32, BNO055 IMU and MyoWare 2.0 sEMG sensor; and a chest module using HealthyPi 5 with the MAX30001 front end for clinical ECG recording.

The current scope is a functional prototype for evaluating shoulder flexion from 0° to 120° in adults aged 18 to 30 with subacromial impingement syndrome. It is intended for early outpatient or home-based rehabilitation under professional supervision.

Objectives

  • Develop a wearable system for objective shoulder rehabilitation using kinematic and sEMG monitoring.
  • Provide immediate feedback to the patient and follow-up data to the physiotherapist.
  • Classify movement performance in real time using green, yellow and red categories based on angle and percentage of maximal voluntary isometric contraction.
  • Store data asynchronously and generate reports including maximum ROM, efficiency ratio and RMS/heart-rate trends.

Technology

🖥️ Hardware

Arm module: ESP32, BNO055 IMU and MyoWare 2.0 sEMG. Chest module: HealthyPi 5 with MAX30001 front end. Wireless BLE star topology.

⚙️ Software

Embedded edge-processing firmware with threshold-based classification, asynchronous SQLite storage and a Unity 3D interface displaying a humanoid model, angle, ROM, repetitions and angular velocity.
