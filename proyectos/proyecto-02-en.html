ARMyo Mirror – Lower Limb

Description

Current Magic Mirror systems mainly display anatomical structures over the real body, but they do not support a dynamic understanding of biomechanical processes. This limits their usefulness for teaching functional biomechanics and movement physiology, particularly in regions such as the knee, hip and foot.

This project develops a hybrid Magic Mirror system combining a real-body mirror view with a virtual anatomical simulation. The user positions the AR window over the joint of interest and activates muscle-contraction animations using gestures from the opposite hand. Flexion and extension are visualized through dynamic muscle models that change shape and color during movement.

Objectives

  • Position the AR window over the joint of interest, including the knee, hip or foot.
  • Activate muscle-contraction animations through gestures performed with the opposite hand.
  • Visualize dynamic muscle models that change shape and color during contraction.
  • Highlight the names of the muscles involved in each phase of movement.

Technology

🖥️ Hardware

Kinect v2 + PC + front-facing monitor.

⚙️ Software

Unity3D + Azure Kinect SDK + Blender.
