Facial Anatomy Simulator

Description

Facial anatomy and neuroanatomy are difficult to learn because facial expressions depend on the coordinated action of underlying muscles, cranial nerves and vascular structures. Traditional methods do not show dynamic muscle activation in real time, limiting understanding of facial biomechanics and disorders such as facial paralysis.

This project develops a mobile augmented-reality application using MediaPipe Face Mesh to detect 468 facial landmarks in real time and overlay interactive anatomical layers on the user’s face.

Objectives

  • Overlay facial muscles such as the masseter, temporalis, buccinator, zygomaticus and orbicularis muscles.
  • Visualize muscle activation during expressions such as smiling, frowning and opening the mouth.
  • Explore the path of the facial nerve and its branches.
  • Include a pathological mode for Bell’s palsy and gamified exercises such as reproducing expressions and locating neurological lesions.

Technology

🖥️ Hardware

Smartphone running iOS or Android.

⚙️ Software

Unity3D + MediaPipe + AR Foundation.
