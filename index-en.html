About Us

BioXR Immersive Lab is a research and development space within the Biomedical Engineering program at Universidad Peruana Cayetano Heredia.

Our team is composed of faculty members, researchers and students dedicated to exploring the potential of immersive technologies—virtual, augmented and mixed reality—applied to healthcare.

We seek to advance educational and clinical innovation through technological solutions that transform anatomy education, clinical simulation, surgical assistance, rehabilitation and telemedicine.

Mission and Vision

Research Areas

Areas where we apply immersive technologies to healthcare

Anatomy Education

We develop immersive applications for anatomy education aimed at health sciences students.

Clinical Simulation

We create immersive environments for safe medical practice using virtual, augmented and mixed reality.

Biomechanics

We visualize muscle contraction and joint movement in real time to support biomechanics learning.

Surgery

We create immersive applications for preoperative planning and intraoperative visualization.

Rehabilitation

We design XR applications for motor and cognitive therapy.

Animal Health / Veterinary Medicine

We extend our immersive solutions to veterinary medical education.

Telemedicine

We investigate new forms of clinician–patient interaction through virtual environments.

Biomedical Instrumentation

We design and integrate sensors and electronics for monitoring and interaction in immersive healthcare systems.
